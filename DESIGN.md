# Design System: Visa Management (Executive Operations)

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Sovereign Portal"**

An authoritative, modern, and beautifully crafted operations platform for visa processing, passport custody, and payment settlements. Clean slate foundations meet an executive sapphire primary tone, enriched with semantic color accents: emerald for delivery & paid state, amber for custody transit and payment requests, violet for visa categories, and ruby for unpaid balances. 

The interface is uncluttered, spacious, purposeful, and free of AI slop templates.

**Core Principles:**
- **Executive Clarity**: Visual hierarchy leads with primary metrics and actionable tasks, eliminating redundant wall-of-text paragraphs.
- **Vibrant Yet Professional Color Palette**: Luminous sapphire brand identity with semantic status pills (emerald, amber, cyan, rose).
- **Dual-Mode Precision**: Seamless transition between crisp porcelain light mode and deep twilight-slate dark mode.
- **Breathing Room**: Spacious padding, balanced typography, and deliberate visual rhythm across landing pages and operational dashboards.
